# NonDenominational — Main Question Index

Status: **working top-level question index**, 2026-11-19.

This file records the 16 established doctrinal categories as parent questions. It is **not** a verbatim reproduction of the prior detailed 92-question inventory (an earlier count of 88 was corrected to 92). The detailed wording and conditional branches must be recovered and reviewed before this index is treated as the canonical questionnaire.

## Main questions by doctrinal category

1. **Prolegomena & Theological Method:** How do you approach theological knowledge, authority, and interpretation?
2. **Bibliology:** What do you believe about Scripture, its inspiration, authority, reliability, and interpretation?
3. **Theology Proper:** What do you believe about God's nature, attributes, knowledge, will, and providence?
4. **Trinitarian Theology:** What do you believe about the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit and their relationship?
5. **Christology:** What do you believe about the identity, person, natures, and work of Jesus Christ?
6. **Pneumatology:** What do you believe about the Holy Spirit's person, work, and gifts?
7. **Anthropology:** What do you believe about humanity, the image of God, human nature, and freedom?
8. **Hamartiology:** What do you believe about sin, the fall, inherited corruption, and human inability?
9. **Soteriology:** How do you understand salvation, grace, election, atonement, faith, justification, and perseverance?
10. **Ecclesiology:** What do you believe the church is, how it should be governed, and who belongs to it?
11. **Sacramentology:** What do you believe about baptism and the Lord's Supper?
12. **Covenant & Biblical Theology:** How do you understand the covenants and continuity between the Testaments?
13. **Eschatology:** What do you believe about death, resurrection, judgment, and the last things?
14. **Worship & Christian Practice:** How should Christians worship and practice their faith together?
15. **Christian Ethics:** How should Christians understand and apply moral obligations?
16. **Spirituality & Christian Life:** How does Christian faith shape sanctification, devotion, and everyday life?

## Question and response architecture

- **Parent question → substantive doctrinal answer → conditional follow-up**, only when additional distinction is relevant.
- Permit **undecided**, **doesn't matter / not important to me**, and **I reject the premise** where those responses make sense; do not attach them indiscriminately to every question.
- Classify the user's actual belief before comparing it to denominational or historical profiles.
- After a substantive answer, present a provisional identification of the belief and pointers for further study. Ask clarifying questions where necessary.
- Surface theological tensions without assuming a tension constitutes a formal contradiction.
- Distinguish sources and explicit doctrinal positions from unspecified positions or permitted diversity.

## Soteriology pilot: established parent-question topics

1. Grace and human participation in salvation.
2. Basis of divine election.
3. Extent and intention of Christ's saving work.
4. Relationship of faith, justification, and works.
5. Whether salvation can be lost.

The current pilot may ask follow-ups about human inability, resistible/effectual grace, and prevenient enabling grace. Logical links among election, grace, atonement, and perseverance are **interpretive relationships**, not automatic deductive proofs.
